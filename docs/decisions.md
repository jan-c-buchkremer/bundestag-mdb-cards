# Decisions

One line per decision, newest last. Date, decision, reason.

- 2026-09-27 — Separate public repository `bundestag-mdb-cards`, a sibling of the foundation and the landscape. The foundation stays UI-free; public like the siblings so the pages can be served from GitHub Pages.
- 2026-09-27 — Two layers for two audiences: a plain card for citizens on top, a detail view for journalists and researchers underneath. The same facts serve both; only the depth differs.
- 2026-09-27 — No code before the open decisions in `plan.md` are settled.
- 2026-09-27 — D1: static site built in Python, published on GitHub Pages, rebuilt daily after `bdf update`. Same path as the landscape, no running service.
- 2026-09-27 — D2: MVP is the card plus Reden, Abstimmungen and Ausschüsse. They need no foundation extension; Drucksachen follows once DIP is ingested.
- 2026-09-27 — D3: topics on the MVP card from DIP `sachgebiet` only; speech topics later from a landscape export. The landscape owns topic modelling.
- 2026-09-27 — D4: foundation extensions in this order: abgeordnetenwatch electoral data and statistics, office periods, structured interjections, photos, page numbers.
- 2026-09-27 — D5: Bundestag portrait photos, subject to a licence check before any is published; the decision is reopened if reuse is not allowed.
- 2026-09-27 — D6: no comparisons on the card; percentiles within the own fraction in the detail layer. The card should not invite ranking.
- 2026-09-27 — D7: German UI, English code and docs, as in the sibling repos.
- 2026-09-27 — D8: constituency list and map first, postcode search later. Postcodes are ambiguous.
- 2026-09-27 — D9: cards for the 635 WP 21 MdBs and for non-MdB speakers (ministers without mandate, Länder representatives), the latter with office and speeches only.
- 2026-09-27 — D10: reuse the landscape's visual language, so the two apps look related.
- 2026-09-27 — D11: full birth date and place, as published in the Stammdaten.
- 2026-09-27 — Pages are rendered in the browser from JSON inlined into each page (as in the landscape), with one shared `card.js` and `cards.css`; Python writes only `<title>`, the meta description and a `<noscript>` line. Each card's JSON is also written next to its page as the export.
- 2026-09-27 — Who gets which card: members are everyone with a WP 21 mandate plus everyone in a WP 21 vote list (Nachrücker after the Stammdaten snapshot 2026-04-29; two of them appear as `is_mdb = 0` in the store). Speakers are everyone else who spoke in a WP 21 sitting, including former MdBs such as a minister without a current mandate. 639 member cards and 14 speaker cards on 2026-09-27.
- 2026-09-27 — A Rede is one `rede` id of the protocol, owned by the person of its first part; parts by other people inside it are their Zwischenfragen or Kurzinterventionen (the chair's words in the preceding part's last six paragraphs decide which, as in the landscape). One question over two parts ("Gestatten Sie …? – Bitte.") counts once when the main speaker said fewer than 30 words in between.
- 2026-09-27 — Regierungsbefragung turns are not Reden: there every question and every answer is its own `rede`, 3,047 of them in WP 21. They are counted separately, as questions (no speaker role) and answers (with a government role).
- 2026-09-27 — Speech length is given in words; the protocol has no speaking time, and estimating minutes would add a number the source does not contain.
- 2026-09-27 — A fraction's line on a roll-call vote is its most common yes/no/abstain vote among its rows (absent excluded); a tie has no line, fraktionslos members have none. A deviation is an own yes/no/abstain vote against an existing line. The rows of all 71 votes add up to the official totals.
- 2026-09-27 — The role-aware context line uses the Stammdaten memberships, which already carry ministers, Parlamentarische Staatssekretäre, Präsidium and fraction leadership with dates; D4 item 2 (office periods) is therefore not needed for the MVP.
- 2026-09-27 — The card uses no gendered pronouns; sentences start with the verb ("Hat 20 Reden …"), the name stands above them.
- 2026-09-27 — Agenda item titles use the landscape's `short_title`, copied into `titles.py`, so a speech carries the same title on both sites.
- 2026-09-27 — Phase 3 spot-check against abgeordnetenwatch.de (independent of the Stammdaten), 10 random members with an aw id: fraction, direct vs. list mandate and Wahlkreis agree for all 10. The only difference is the Stammdaten's abbreviated name of Wahlkreis 60 ("BRB a.d.Havel– …"). It also confirms that a list member's Wahlkreis in the Stammdaten is where they stood as a candidate, which the card states.
- 2026-09-27 — The card states Drucksachen counts only when DIP has Drucksachen in every month with a sitting; until then the tab shows what is loaded with an "incomplete" note. A partial backfill would otherwise read as "no Anträge".
- 2026-09-27 — Container image and CI as in the landscape (stdlib only, 187 MB); CI builds and pushes on `main` only. The compose service, `update.sh` line and Pages publishing are written down in `docs/deploy.md` but not applied: they need a merge, a push and changes in `infra`.
- 2026-09-27 — A Rede needs at least 500 characters (re-joined per speaker), the landscape's MIN_CHARS. Shorter contributions (113 in WP 21: oaths, one-sentence statements) are listed in the Reden tab as "Kurze Wortbeiträge" and not counted. Every Rede now also exists on the landscape map.
- 2026-09-27 — Election details come from the foundation's Bundeswahlleiterin tables (foundation PR #3): how a member was elected, the Wahlkreis won or stood in with the own first-vote share, and the list position. A list member's share is the party's Erststimme result in that Wahlkreis. Without those tables the cards fall back to the Stammdaten line, so the build does not depend on the foundation version.
- 2026-09-27 — The Wahlkreis view lists all 299 Wahlkreise from the official file and says, for the 23 without a direct member, which party had the most first votes and that they were not covered by second votes. It uses the official Wahlkreis names; the Stammdaten abbreviate some ("BRB a.d.Havel– …").
- 2026-09-27 — Land codes of the Stammdaten before WP 18 (NDS, BAY, THÜ, … and Baden, Württemberg-Baden, Württemberg-Hohenzollern, Berlin (West)) are mapped to names in the Laufbahn tab.
- 2026-09-28 — Citizen questions from abgeordnetenwatch (foundation `aw_profile`) on the card: "Hat auf abgeordnetenwatch.de 95 von 107 Bürgerfragen beantwortet", marked as lifetime totals of the profile with their date. 119 of 630 profiles have questions but no answered count; those cards give the number of questions and say that the platform gives no answered count, instead of showing 0. Quellen links the public profile instead of the API record.
- 2026-09-28 — "Im Plenum" tab from the foundation's `interjection` table: per fraction, the applause (whole fraction / some members), Zurufe, laughter and dissent the protocol records during the member's own contributions, and the member's own named Zwischenrufe with their words. Labelled as notes *during* a contribution, not reactions *to* it: the protocol records when applause falls, not whom it is for (applause from Die Linke during an AfD speech usually greets a heckler). For the same reason the card has no applause sentence. Zwischenrufe carry only the sitting; the page builds the protocol link, which kept the heaviest page at 850 KB instead of 1 MB. JSON exports are written compact (site 127 MB instead of 146 MB).
- 2026-09-28 — Seating chart (`parliament.js`, shared by the index and the vote pages) drawn like the bundestag.de Sitzverteilung: fraction wedges in the house's order (AfD, CDU/CSU, Grüne, SPD, Die Linke, fraktionslos, seen from the Präsidium), each wedge's angle proportional to its seats, all wedges on the same concentric rows and every row filled in proportion to its length, so the dots form even arcs. Inside a wedge the fraction leadership sits in the front row, the rest by Land and name, swept radially so a Land reads as a Landesgruppe block. The real seat plan is not published as data, so this is a diagram of the fractions, not of who sits where. Touch: first tap shows the name, second tap opens; keyboard: one Tab stop, arrow keys move between seats.
- 2026-09-28 — The index opens on a Plenum view with the seating chart; Abgeordnete and Wahlkreise are the other two views. The list's search, Land, mandate, committee and chip filters also filter the seats (non-matches faded), so the chart and the list never disagree. Only sitting members have a seat; former members and non-members stay in the list.
- 2026-09-28 — "Redner:innen der letzten Sitzung": the owners of each rede in the latest sitting in the store (Regierungsbefragung turns included, Zwischenfragen not), with a link to that ISO week in the Themenlandschaft. From `data.last_sitting`.
- 2026-09-28 — Government from the foundation's `government_role` (Wikidata): Kanzler, Minister, Staatsminister and Parlamentarische Staatssekretäre with an open or future end date sit on the Regierungsbank and carry a "Regierung" badge and filter; beamtete Staatssekretäre are civil servants and are left out. MdB ministers keep their fraction seat as well, so the wedges still add up to the house. Without the table the index builds as before, with no Regierungsbank people.
- 2026-09-28 — Wahlkreis map from the Bundeswahlleiterin's generalised shapefile (UTM32), simplified once with shapely's coverage simplification at 250 m so neighbours keep a shared border, as SVG paths in kilometres (266 KB, 77 KB gzipped). It is loaded with fetch only when the Wahlkreise view opens, so the inlined index stays small. Fill by the party that got the seat; grey where the winner had no Zweitstimmendeckung. Attribution as the licence (dl-de/by-2-0) asks, under the map and in the README.
- 2026-09-28 — Portraits from the foundation's `person_photo` (bundestag.de biographies, Commons for some non-MdB government members) are shown without a licence check, as decided for round 2, always with the credit ("Foto: …", linked to the source page). The build downscales them to `fotos/<id>.jpg`, 240 px wide, JPEG q80 (Pillow, threaded); a manifest (`fotos/.manifest.json`) keeps the source path, size and mtime, so only new or changed photos are re-encoded (dev store: 644 photos, 8.3 MB, 1.9 s cold, 0.02 s warm). The raw folder is `BDF_RAW`, else `raw/` next to the store. Cards without a photo show initials.
- 2026-09-28 — Abstimmungen tab: each row leads with the overall result (angenommen/abgelehnt as announced in the protocol, foundation `decision`; without a decision row a counted simple majority of Ja over Nein, a tie rejects) and the house's counts, links to its vote page `abstimmungen/<id with - for />.html`, then the own vote; the fraction comparison is a small marker ("wie Fraktion" / "abweichend"). Three summary tiles replace the per-row fraction bar.
- 2026-09-28 — Every person in `government_role` gets a card: MdBs keep theirs with an office block, others get a speaker card (kind unchanged, so the index needs no new kind) with offices, speeches and Regierungsbefragung, and only the tabs that have data. Rows naming the same office for an overlapping time are merged, dated by Wikidata, then Stammdaten, then protocol. Protocol rows are evidence only: shown as "laut Plenarprotokoll, zuletzt belegt am <date>" (see below), and their last date does not end the office (also on the Regierungsbank). Wikidata's masculine office labels are made feminine for women (gender, or a feminine protocol role).
- 2026-09-28 — Sitting pages: an agenda item with more than 10 speeches shows the first 10, fades the list out over a row of the other speakers' faces and opens the rest with "alle N Reden zeigen" (closing again keeps the button in place). Every row stays in the HTML, so all card links remain in the file; without JavaScript the list is fully open.
- 2026-09-28 — "Worum ging es" per agenda item: the Themenlandschaft clusters of the item's speeches, most speeches first, top 5, each linking to `<week>.html#cluster=<id>`. Read from the landscape's `speech_clusters.json` (`LANDSCAPE_CLUSTERS`), keyed by the foundation's speech id as the `#open=` links; the landscape owns topic modelling (D3). Without the file the block is left out and the build succeeds.
- 2026-09-28 — An agenda item's short title comes from its first sub-item only: in "a) Entwurf eines Gesetzes … | b) … zu dem Antrag … | <Antrag title>" the law is non-procedural only inside its first segment, so the old rule took the motion's title from b) (105 of 877 WP 21 items). A new sub-item starts at a procedural segment beginning "b)", "25 b)", "ZP 3" or "2 …". The landscape's copy of `short_title` needs the same change to keep both sites in step.
- 2026-09-28 — Government colour is a neutral slate, `--reg: #64748b` (badge text `#334155` on `#e2e8f0`), for the "Regierung" badge, its chip dot and Regierungsbank seats of members without a fraction. The earlier gold (`#a08a2e`) read as brown and as a party colour; slate is no party's colour, stays distinct from the CDU/CSU black and the grey of fraktionslos (`#8a8a8a`), and keeps enough contrast on white (4.8:1) and on a dark card (3.5:1 on `#1d1d21`). The site has no dark theme of its own; the tokens are named so one can override them.
- 2026-09-28 — Seat highlight (speakers of the last sitting, search and chip filters): every other seat fades to 25 % and the highlighted seats get a thin halo in the accent colour, with a gap of background colour between seat and halo. The former thick dark ring looked heavy and disappeared on the CDU/CSU black; the halo with its gap reads on black, coloured and light seats alike. Hover, focus and a tapped seat grow by 35 % and show the halo in the text colour. Seats are drawn at 0.68 of the seat spacing so there is a clear gap even when grown. The chart picks its dark palette from the background it sits on (luminance of the first opaque ancestor), not from `prefers-color-scheme`, because the pages themselves are light: the old media query put light rims and dark empty seats onto a white card.
- 2026-09-28 — The Regierungsbank seats the whole current government from `government_role` (Kanzler, Minister, Staatsminister, Parlamentarische Staatssekretäre), including roles only the protocols show. Such a role counts as current; its tooltip and the card's office block say "laut Plenarprotokoll, zuletzt belegt am <date>" instead of an end date (`data.government` returns `evidence` and `seen`). Seats without a person have a tooltip too: Bundesrat ("nicht Teil dieses Datensatzes"), free Regierungsbank and Präsidium seats, the stenographers. `feminine()` now changes only the holder's title ("Staatsministerin beim Bundeskanzler", not "… beim Bundeskanzlerin").
- 2026-09-28 — Wahlkreis map: hover and selection get an accent outline on a casing of the card colour plus a soft shadow, and hover brightens the region, instead of the black hover outline that vanished on CDU/CSU regions. Zoom and pan change the viewBox (strokes do not scale, the aspect ratio stays, so the phone layout keeps its height): mouse wheel, pinch, drag, +/−/⟲ buttons, up to 24×; picking a Wahlkreis in the list (or opening a `#…&wk=` link) zooms to it. On touch the map lets vertical swipes scroll the page until it is zoomed in, then one finger pans the map.
- 2026-09-28 — Card tabs: the bar scrolls sideways (touch, trackpad, and a vertical wheel over it) with a fade and chevron on the side that has more tabs; the active tab is scrolled into view. Switching tabs no longer follows the anchor: the page stays where it is, or, when the bar is stuck at the top, the new tab starts right under it; the tab body keeps at least a viewport of height so a short tab cannot pull the page up.
- 2026-09-29 — Geschlossenheit (`abstimmungen/geschlossenheit.html`, `cohesion.py`): the fraction line is the one the vote pages and cards already use (`data.majority`: most common of Ja/Nein/Enthaltung, none on a tie = "gespalten", none for fraktionslos); a dissent is a cast Ja/Nein/Enthaltung other than the line, never absent or invalid. Cohesion per fraction and vote is the Rice index |Ja − Nein| / (Ja + Nein), abstentions left out of the value (they still count as dissent), shown as small multiples in inline SVG with the average dashed; the average has three decimals and is never rounded up to 1. All dissents are listed newest first with links to vote and card, filterable by fraction; no counts per member, so no "most rebellious" list. Vote pages ("Anders als die Fraktionsmehrheit") and cards ("abweichend", counter, filter) already showed dissents, so they are unchanged.
